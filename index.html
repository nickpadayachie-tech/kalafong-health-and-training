What Is Occupational Health Screening?

Occupational health screening involves health assessments carried out to help determine whether an employee is physically and mentally suited to the requirements of their specific job duties. Employers use these workplace health assessments to check employee wellbeing, monitor ongoing health and support workplace safety.

Screening can help assess fitness for specific work duties and can be conducted at the client's premises where offered. Assessment requirements may vary according to the employee's role, workplace and occupational health needs.

Pre-Employment

Pre-Employment Screening

Pre-employment screening involves appropriate health assessments before an employee starts work, according to the requirements of the role. These pre-employment screenings help employers and occupational health professionals assess whether a candidate is physically and mentally suited to the demands of the position.

Depending on the requirements of the role and the occupational health assessment, appropriate tests may include a physical examination, medical history review and other relevant checks. Not every candidate will receive every test - assessments are tailored to the role and workplace requirements.

Periodic

Periodic & Annual Employee Screening

Periodic and annual employee health screening is carried out to support ongoing workplace health monitoring for existing employees. Regular checks help employers and occupational health professionals monitor employee wellbeing over time and identify occupational health concerns early.

These periodic screenings support workplace health monitoring so that any changes in an employee's health can be followed up appropriately. Assessment requirements may vary according to the employee's role, workplace and occupational health needs.

Exit

Exit Medical Screening

Exit medical screening is carried out for employees leaving an organisation, with appropriate occupational health assessment and documentation. It provides a professional record of the employee's health status at the time of leaving and helps identify any work-related conditions where applicable.

Exit screening requirements may vary according to the workplace and circumstances. We provide clear, professional documentation to support your organisation.

Return to Work

Return-to-Work Screening

Return-to-work health assessments are carried out to help determine readiness for employees returning after illness or injury. These assessments provide information that can support a safe return to the workplace.

Return-to-work screening is designed to support the return process rather than to make medical decisions about an employee's condition. Assessment requirements may vary according to the employee's role, workplace and occupational health needs.

Occupational Health Medical Tests
