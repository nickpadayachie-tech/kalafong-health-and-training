Exit Medical Screening

Occupational health assessments for employees leaving an organisation

Exit medical screening is carried out for employees leaving an organisation, with appropriate occupational health assessment and documentation. It provides a professional record of the employee's health status at the time of leaving and helps identify any work-related conditions where applicable.

Exit screening requirements may vary according to the workplace and circumstances. We provide clear, professional documentation to support your organisation.

Exit medical assessments help employers close out the occupational health relationship professionally and provide a confidential record of the employee's health at the point of departure.

What's Included

What Exit Medical Screening Includes

Exit medical screening typically includes the following, depending on the requirements of the role, the workplace and the circumstances of departure:

  • Physical examination where applicable
  • Medical history review
  • Fitness at exit assessment
  • Professional medical documentation
  • Occupational health certification where required

Exit screening requirements may vary according to the workplace and circumstances. We provide clear, professional documentation to support your organisation.

Why It Matters

Why Exit Screening Matters

Exit medical screening provides a professional, documented record of an employee's health status when they leave an organisation. This can help protect both the employer and the employee, and provides useful information where any work-related condition needs to be considered.

A professional exit assessment also supports the employer's workplace health and safety obligations and helps maintain good occupational health practice throughout the full employment lifecycle.
