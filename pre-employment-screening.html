Pre-Employment Screening

Occupational health assessments for new employees in Qwaqwa and the Free State

Pre-employment screening involves appropriate health assessments before an employee starts work, according to the requirements of the role. These pre-employment screenings help employers and occupational health professionals assess whether a candidate is physically and mentally suited to the demands of the position.

Depending on the requirements of the role and the occupational health assessment, appropriate tests may include a physical examination, medical history review and other relevant checks. Not every candidate will receive every test â€” assessments are tailored to the role and workplace requirements.

By conducting pre-employment screening before a new employee starts, employers can support workplace safety, reduce potential workplace risks and confirm that candidates are fit for the duties they will perform.

What’s Included

What Pre-Employment Screening Includes

Pre-employment screening at Kalafong Health & Training typically includes the following checks, depending on the requirements of the role and the occupational health assessment:

  • Physical examination
  • Medical history review
  • Fitness for work assessment
  • Vision screening (where required)
  • Body mass index (BMI) assessment
  • Audiometry, spirometry and laboratory tests where relevant to the role

Not every candidate receives every test. The appropriate tests depend on the requirements of the role and the occupational health assessment. Assessment requirements may vary according to the employee's role, workplace and occupational health needs.

Why It Matters

Why Pre-Employment Screening Matters

Pre-employment medical screening helps employers establish a baseline of each employee's health at the start of the working relationship. This supports workplace safety, helps identify whether a candidate is physically suited to the demands of the role, and can assist with managing occupational health requirements over time.

Occupational health screening carried out before employment begins also helps employers meet their workplace health and safety obligations and provides professional documentation of the assessment that was conducted.
