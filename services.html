What Is Occupational Health Screening?

Occupational health screening involves health assessments carried out to help determine whether an employee is physically and mentally suited to the requirements of their specific job duties. Employers use these workplace health assessments to check employee wellbeing, monitor ongoing health and support workplace safety.

Screening can help assess fitness for specific work duties and can be conducted at the client’s premises where offered. Assessment requirements may vary according to the employee’s role, workplace and occupational health needs.

👔

Pre-Employment Screening

Pre-employment health assessments for new employees before they start work, according to the requirements of the role. Helps assess employee fitness for work.

  • Physical examination
